# Design Principles

- Contracts over prompts: the analytical claim and its constraints should be explicit.
- Audit before render: the chart should be inspected before it is shared.
- Explain warnings: findings should be deterministic and understandable.
- Human remains responsible for analytical judgment: the package flags risks, it does not replace review.
- No auto-fix in v0.1: the tool surfaces issues but does not silently rewrite the chart.
- Tufte-inspired, not Tufte-certified: v0.1 borrows practical integrity ideas without claiming certification.
